# MyRecon OSINT article and keyword map

Reviewed: 8 October 2026. Scope: twelve original blog articles, written for distinct research questions and integrated with existing guides and product pages.

## Evidence and keyword method

These are candidate search phrases based on the user's requested topics, ordinary question wording, existing site coverage and current official tool documentation. They are not measured search volumes, customer-query analytics or guaranteed ranking opportunities. No Search Console query export or paid keyword dataset was available for this writing task. Do not label a phrase “high volume” or “most searched” without separate evidence.

Use one clear primary intent per page. Related phrases belong in useful explanations, headings and specific FAQs; they should not become a repeated keyword block or many overlapping pages. The new articles add selection criteria, workflows, accessible comparison tables, source links, quick answers and exact visible FAQ text. Dates reflect the actual source review on 8 October 2026.

## MyRecon capability review

The article writer inspected the working source based on `origin/main`, including:

- `backend/services/search.py`: public username pipeline; `coverage`, `unverified`, `platform_checks`, `partial` and source error fields in relevant workflows.
- `frontend/assets/js/app.js`: guest/sign-in/plan gates, scan coverage and unverified-result display, CSV/JSON export routines.
- `frontend/index.html`: supported public-tool descriptions and the recorded comparison methodology, including its one-username case and restricted daily benchmark.
- Existing privacy, self-audit, evidence, account-match and false-positive guides.

No new live username scans, private-account access, overall accuracy result or global performance ranking are claimed. Runtime provider availability and production deployment identity require the release owner's live checks. MyRecon's article descriptions consistently retain access and incomplete-evidence limits.

## AEO/GEO content contract

Every new source article starts with a direct quick answer, includes a captioned table with explicit headers, offers a distinct procedure and has three visible FAQs. Metadata `faq` uses `{question, answer}` matching visible text exactly. Top10 metadata `tools` uses `{name, url, description}` in the same visible order; every tool URL occurs in a visible link. `datePublished` and `dateModified` are `2026-10-08`.

Schema and crawler discovery make content easier to interpret; they do not establish search indexing, ranking, featured-answer eligibility or citations by AI systems. Do not add unsupported review scores, aggregate ratings, awards or independent-ranking language. The top10 #3 placement is explicitly the publisher's editorial selection, with selection basis and a conflict disclosure above the list and a matching FAQ below it.

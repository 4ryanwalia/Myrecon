# MyRecon independent coverage

The former backlink and syndication templates are retired. They included
unsupported platform-count and account-count claims, ranking assumptions, and
outreach without sufficiently clear affiliation disclosures.

Use the [independent review kit](../review-kit/README.md) and
[verified outreach tracker and drafts](independent-coverage-outreach.md).

Request honest testing with reproducible evidence and a visible affiliation
disclosure. A submission, self-authored article or directory entry must not be
presented as an independent review. Publication and conclusions belong to the
recipient; no backlink, rating, endorsement or positive coverage is a condition.

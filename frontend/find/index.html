Review your public footprint with MyRecon

Use the app for a broader self-audit of supported public usernames. Availability differs by platform, and a matching handle does not establish who owns an account. The Android app runs a username sweep from your phone and labels uncertain checks as unknown.

Core app tools are free and need no signup. The Google Play listing is in closed testing, so only eligible testers can access it until public release. View MyRecon on Google Play.

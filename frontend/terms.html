7. Accuracy, and what results are not

Results are assembled from third-party public sources in real time. They may be incomplete, out of date, mistaken, or about a different person with the same name or handle. A username matching on a platform does not prove the same person owns both accounts, and an absence of results does not prove an absence of exposure.

Results are not: a background check, a credit or consumer report, a criminal-records check, an identity verification, legal advice, or evidence. Do not treat them as any of those. Verify anything that matters at its source before you act on it, and never take an adverse decision about a person on the strength of a MyRecon result alone.

The password checker tells you whether a password appears in known breach corpora. It is not an assessment of whether any particular account of yours is safe.

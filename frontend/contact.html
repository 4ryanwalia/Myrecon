Privacy requests

If you sign in, we keep your email, name, plan and scan counts, plus up to your 50 newest completed username scans so you can reopen them. You can delete any scan or all of them from your account on the pricing page. Other lookups may be held briefly in the API cache, but are not saved to your account history. See the privacy policy for exactly what is and is not retained. If you believe something about you is being held, write and say so.
aryan@bugsnaps.in

Security disclosure

Found a vulnerability in MyRecon? Please report it privately before disclosing it publicly, and we will confirm receipt and keep you updated on the fix. Testing is welcome against our own systems only.
aryan@bugsnaps.in

What we cannot help with

We do not run investigations on third parties on request, and we cannot help you access, recover or monitor an account that is not yours. Requests of that kind are declined without exception, see our terms. If you are dealing with harassment or impersonation, contact the platform involved and your local authorities; we can advise on gathering evidence about your own accounts.

What Intelligence X is designed to do

Intelligence X operates as an archival search engine that indexes historical pastebin dumps, historical web archives, dark web forums, and data leaks. It serves cybersecurity researchers, intelligence analysts, and fraud investigators who require historical snapshots and raw document analysis.

Where a MyRecon workflow fits

MyRecon focuses on live, client-side username intelligence and personal digital footprint auditing across 500+ active platforms. It empowers individual users and authorized researchers to quickly map public profiles, evaluate email breach metadata, analyze DNS/WHOIS records, and perform consent-based footprint cleanups.

Compare evidence rather than headline numbers

IntelX searches archival data dumps, while MyRecon queries live platform APIs and endpoints. Always verify findings against primary source documentation and maintain a clear separation between historical records and currently active accounts.

Check a public username

Use accounts and identifiers you own or are authorized to review. A matching username is a lead, not proof of identity.

Frequently asked questions

Is MyRecon a free alternative to Intelligence X?

For live social media username searches, email exposure checks, and domain/DNS audits, MyRecon is completely free for standard scans. For historical pastebin and dark web document archiving, IntelX provides specialized archival indexing.

Can I search emails on both tools?

Yes. IntelX returns documents where the email is referenced historically. MyRecon queries verified breach metadata to show confirmed security incidents, compromised data categories, and recommended remediation steps.

Does MyRecon index the dark web?

No. MyRecon exclusively queries public, clearnet social platforms, forums, registries, and ethical breach disclosure feeds under standard open-source intelligence principles.

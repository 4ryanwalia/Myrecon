Competitor details: BeenVerified official source

What BeenVerified documents

The publisher describes its app as a way to search available public records by name and look up phone numbers. The linked publisher listing supports this description. We did not buy or test a report.

What MyRecon checks and what a result means

MyRecon checks public usernames across a supported catalogue of 500+ platforms. The chosen scan scope determines which checks run. Found means a lead for source review. Not found does not guarantee registration availability. Unknown means the check could not reach a reliable conclusion. Email and password-exposure tools are separate checks, not a complete inventory of every account.

Illustrative decision: A public-record entry and an old gaming handle

For a forgotten gaming profile, start with the handle and your saved account records. A people-search record answers a different question. Review the original record before relying on it; do not connect a record to a profile merely because names or photos resemble each other.

Access and costs before you choose

MyRecon guest Quick scans show a limited preview. Standard scans require sign-in; account and plan limits apply. Paid features are described on the current pricing page. Check BeenVerified provider terms and available fields separately. This page does not claim either service is unlimited, always free or anonymous, and includes no paid-account or same-scope benchmark.

Record evidence and verify your next action

For each authorized check, record the exact input, date, scope, result status and source URL. Keep unknown outcomes separate from negative results. Confirm accounts you own through an existing login or official recovery process, apply the privacy or security change, and recheck the specific exposure. A shared username alone does not identify a person.

Frequently asked questions

Is MyRecon a direct replacement for BeenVerified?

It depends on the task. Public profile discovery and public-record research have different inputs and evidence limits. Compare the required workflow before switching.

Can I try MyRecon for free?

Guest Quick scans offer a limited preview. Standard scans require sign-in, and account and plan limits apply. See the current pricing page.

Does a match prove ownership?

No. A shared username, similar name or historical record is not ownership proof. Keep uncertain matches unconfirmed and review independent evidence.

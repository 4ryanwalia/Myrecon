How to use it

  1. Enter a handle, full name or exact email you own or have permission to investigate. For names and handles, add context after a comma to improve relevance.
  2. Press Investigate to check public sources. Guests see the returned sections with finding details locked. Sign in with an eligible paid account to reveal the retained investigation.
  3. Open each source before making a connection. Check unavailable and partial-source notices; empty results do not establish absence.
What Deep Search can find

Handle searches ask GitHub, Hacker News, Reddit, Bluesky, mastodon.social and DEV for public accounts and recent activity, and Keybase for published account proofs. Name searches ask Wikidata, GitHub's full-name index and ORCID for candidates. Indexed-web queries cover professional pages, social profiles, public discussions, news, research, records and documents.

Instagram and other source limits

Instagram results are indexed profiles, captions, tagged or mentioned pages and external links. Instagram comment bodies, likes and private content are unavailable. Search engines and platforms may block or rate-limit this server; unavailable sources never count as absent.

Google contributor profiles and reviews

Email searches use the exact email to ask the configured public Google source for a contributor profile, then collect available public Maps reviews for that contributor. Review cards can include the place, address, rating, date, text and an owner reply when returned. Coverage counts describe what the source returned and may be partial or capped. Profile edits do not establish online activity, and no returned profile or reviews does not prove that a Google account or activity does not exist.

Privacy and data handling

Your query is processed by the website server and sent to public platforms, registries and search providers. Guest investigations are retained in server memory for up to 30 minutes so an eligible account can reveal findings after sign-in; a server restart can remove them sooner. Guests receive section metadata and placeholders, with finding details withheld by the server. A temporary report token and this limited preview stay in your browser session to support sign-in redirects. Results are not saved to your account by this feature. Downloaded reports stay on your device. Sign-in and abuse-prevention data follow our privacy policy. Opening a browser query link sends that query to Google.

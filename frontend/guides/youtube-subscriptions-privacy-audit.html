YouTube subscriptions privacy audit

View on Google Play

Your YouTube channel can reveal an interest trail through subscriptions, playlists, and public activity. Subscription visibility has its own setting and exceptions, so a casual look at the channel home page is not enough. This self-audit starts with Google's official YouTube controls and ends with a careful check of where your public channel handle appears elsewhere.

Key takeaways

  • Verify your YouTube subscription visibility in Settings and Privacy rather than relying on the default described in Help.
  • Subscriber-only interactions can reveal a subscription even when your full list is private, so consider the audience of each action.
  • Review playlists separately and use MyRecon only to check public reuse of the channel handle, validating each original result.

Check the subscription setting

YouTube Help says subscriptions are private by default and documents the Keep all my subscriptions private control under Settings and Privacy on a computer. Verify your current choice rather than assuming the default still applies to an older account. If subscriptions are public, they can appear on the channel home page and in a channel's subscriber list.

Know the visible participation exception

Even with subscriptions set to private, YouTube says other viewers may see that you subscribe when you take part in a subscriber-only live chat or comment on a video or Short restricted to subscribers and members. Consider this before using the account in a context where you want to keep a subscription association private. This is different from showing your full subscription list.

Review channel and playlist surfaces

Open your own channel in a signed-out browser and check its name, handle, description, links, and visible playlists. YouTube manages playlist visibility separately, so review any playlist you publish through its own settings. Record exactly which page was visible and avoid drawing conclusions from a personalized signed-in view alone.

Check your handle across public sites

MyRecon can help you search the public username used by your channel. Open candidate results at their original sites, since a matching handle does not prove they are your accounts. Its Android app is free and needs no signup; the Google Play listing is currently in closed testing, which may limit installation until release.

Check a public username

Use accounts and identifiers you own or are authorized to review. A matching username is a lead, not proof of identity.

Frequently asked questions

Are YouTube subscriptions private by default?

YouTube's help page says subscription privacy settings are private by default. Still inspect your own account's Settings and Privacy page because an existing account may have been changed and privacy choices are specific to the account.

Can someone see that I subscribe in a live chat?

Yes, in a subscriber-only live chat or a comment area limited to subscribers and members, YouTube says other viewers can see your subscription participation even when your subscription list is private.

Does a private subscription setting hide public playlists?

No. Subscription privacy and playlist visibility are different controls. Inspect each playlist you manage and its visibility setting, then check your signed-out channel view to see what is actually displayed.

Discord profile privacy is more specific than a single public-or-private switch. Its profile audience setting controls details such as a bio and connected accounts, while some identifiers remain visible and activity sharing has a separate control. This guide walks through a practical review of your own account and explains why a username match elsewhere is only a lead.

Key takeaways

  • Set a Discord profile audience in Data & Privacy and remember that username, display name, and some basic details remain visible.
  • Review Activity Privacy and displayed connections separately because changing profile privacy does not automatically settle every sharing choice.
  • Use MyRecon only to inspect public username references and verify the original page before drawing any identity conclusion.

Choose who sees your full profile

Discord documents a Profile Privacy setting under User Settings and Data & Privacy on desktop and mobile. Review the available audience choices, including Friends Only and server-based options, and pick the narrowest audience that fits your purpose. Check the official help page again if the interface changes; account settings can differ across clients and age groups.

Check what remains visible

Discord says profile privacy can hide a bio, pronouns, badges, connected accounts, and other details, but the username, display name, avatar, account age, and some shared-server context remain visible. Look at your own profile through an account with the relevant relationship, where permitted, rather than assuming all profile fields disappear. Avoid gathering private details about other server members.

Review activity sharing separately

Discord states Profile Privacy and Activity Sharing are separate settings. If you reduce the audience for one, review the other under Activity Privacy so your game or app activity matches your intent. Also examine any account connections you chose to display. A public cross-platform connection is stronger evidence than two unrelated matching handles.

Audit the handle you use elsewhere

Check your own Discord username with MyRecon's public username search and inspect each original result before linking it to you. The Android app is free and requires no signup, although its Google Play listing is currently in closed testing and installation may be restricted. If you want less linkage, change public profile details or reuse choices you control.

Check a public username

Use accounts and identifiers you own or are authorized to review. A matching username is a lead, not proof of identity.

Frequently asked questions

Does Discord Profile Privacy hide my username?

No. Discord's documentation lists username and display name among the details that remain visible when profile privacy is enabled. The setting narrows access to fuller profile details; it is not a promise of complete anonymity.

Is activity sharing covered by the same setting?

Discord describes Profile Privacy and Activity Sharing as separate settings. Review Activity Privacy after changing your profile audience to make sure the visibility of your activity also matches your choice.

Can a username search see a private Discord profile?

A public username search should not be treated as a way to access private profile details. It may surface public references to the same text, but each result needs direct verification and a shared handle does not prove common ownership.

Separating confirmed matches from false positives

Automated tools classify each source into found, not_found, or unknown. Login walls, CAPTCHAs, and rate limits produce unknown rather than a fake clean result. Always open the source URL and corroborate profile photos, bio links, or timestamps before linking two accounts.

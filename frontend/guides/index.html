Choose an outcome before collecting data

For your own accounts, start with the digital-footprint checklist and username workflow. For a public-source report, use account-match verification and evidence logging. For a team, protect research notes before sharing screenshots, and use the brand-impersonation workflow when a lookalike makes a misleading claim. A larger collection of profile links is not a substitute for a verified account action or a supportable finding.

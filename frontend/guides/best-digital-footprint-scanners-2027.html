Why false-positive and unknown handling matters in 2027

Many legacy scanners treat any HTTP 200 status as a confirmed account, flooding reports with soft-404s and generic signup pages. Modern OSINT tools separate found, not_found, and unknown outcomes (rate limits, login walls, CAPTCHAs) so you never confuse a blocked request with a clean footprint.

Privacy and data handling disclosures

01

Search Data

Live public-platform checks, with Google dorking on standard scans when configured. We do not create search-query logs or sell search data.

Results may be cached in memory for 10 minutes. Signed-in username reports and browser search history are retained. IP-based rate limits, daily keyed IP hashes and hosting request logs support abuse prevention.

02

Account Data

Sign-in keeps your account ID, email, display name, creation time, scan allowances, plan/payment references and saved username reports.

We never sell personal data or share it for another party's marketing. Authentication, hosting and payment providers process account data. Google Analytics and advertising may process IP addresses and cookies; our analytics configuration excludes search values.

03

API Lookups

LeakCheck email lookups run over HTTPS, on the fly, without application caching or account database retention.

Other email sources receive the identifiers needed to answer a lookup. Each provider has its own privacy policy. Recent searches and investigations you save can remain in this browser.

Free OSINT tool for ethical research and digital footprint audits

MyRecon provides a free, browser-based OSINT (Open-Source Intelligence) investigation tool designed for privacy researchers, security analysts, and individuals auditing their online exposure. Run instant public sweeps across 500+ platforms without installing Python CLI scripts or complex dependencies.

Our OSINT framework combines multi-platform reverse username searches, email breach exposure checks, password leak detection (via k-anonymity), domain WHOIS lookups, and DNS mail-security auditing in a single private interface. Every result is classified with evidence-backed states: found, not found, or unknown due to rate limits or login gates.

Free digital footprint checker & online exposure audit

Your digital footprint is the collection of all public data associated with your name, usernames, email addresses, and online accounts. MyRecon’s free digital footprint checker unifies reverse username lookups across 500+ platforms, historical data breach searches, and leaked credential audits into a single privacy-first dashboard.

Conducting regular footprint audits helps you discover forgotten profiles, identify leaked credentials before they lead to account takeover, and eliminate stale information exposed to data brokers.

A username checker for your public digital footprint

Common questions

What is a reverse username lookup and how does it work?

A reverse username lookup queries public endpoint URLs across 500+ social networks, developer communities, and forums to determine where a specific handle or alias is registered. Read our reverse username lookup guide.

What is a digital footprint check?

A digital footprint check audits your public online exposure by scanning registered usernames, checking whether your email was involved in data breaches, and identifying leaked credentials. See our digital footprint audit guide.

Is MyRecon free to use?

MyRecon offers free guest Quick scans across 100 platforms, with no sign-up required. Only the first 2 result cards are visible; remaining cards are blurred. Standard scans across 500+ platforms require sign-in. Paid plans add Deep Search and Extended scans. See the current pricing page for features and limits.

More common questions (5)
Does a username lookup confirm availability?

No. MyRecon checks public profile presence. A missing profile does not guarantee that a handle can be registered. Confirm username availability through the official platform signup or account settings flow.

What is a social media username checker?

A social media username checker looks for public profiles using a handle across supported platforms. MyRecon helps you review those profile leads for your own digital footprint or authorized research. Verify important matches at the source.

What can I check with MyRecon?

MyRecon includes public username, email and breach, password exposure, domain registration, DNS, and IP checks. Coverage and access can vary by tool, so use the live interface and current plan page to confirm what is available.

Does a missing username result prove no account exists?

No. A site may block, rate-limit, rename, or restrict a profile, and some services may not be supported. Treat an unavailable or missing result as inconclusive unless the source provides a verifiable reason.

Can MyRecon confirm that a profile belongs to a person?

No. A matching username is only a lead. Confirm important conclusions with independent public evidence and keep uncertain links unconfirmed. MyRecon does not access private accounts or restricted content.

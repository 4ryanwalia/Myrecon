Common questions

What can I check with MyRecon?

MyRecon includes public username, email and breach, password exposure, domain registration, DNS, and IP checks. Coverage and access can vary by tool, so use the live interface and current plan page to confirm what is available.

Does a missing username result prove no account exists?

No. A site may block, rate-limit, rename, or restrict a profile, and some services may not be supported. Treat an unavailable or missing result as inconclusive unless the source provides a verifiable reason.

Can I run a username search across social media with MyRecon?

MyRecon checks usernames against supported public profile sources across social, developer, and community platforms. Coverage varies by platform; a blocked or inconclusive check is not proof that a profile is absent.

Does an online username lookup confirm who owns an account?

No. MyRecon’s per-platform validation helps keep false positives low, but no username search is 100% accurate or proves the same person controls multiple profiles. Treat a match as a lead and verify it with independent public evidence.

Can MyRecon find private or restricted profiles?

Sometimes. MyRecon can find a profile and show limited details a platform makes publicly available, even when the profile is marked private. It cannot view private posts, messages, or other restricted content. Coverage varies by platform, so checks may be inconclusive.

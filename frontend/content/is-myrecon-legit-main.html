Is MyRecon legit? Six checks you can review

Evaluate a public-source tool by its result evidence, access limits, data handling and current offer. MyRecon produces candidates for source review; it cannot certify that two accounts belong to one person or that no information exists online.

1. Check the source behind a result

Open a reported profile URL and confirm it resolves to the expected account page. A login form, platform home page or generic application shell is not proof of an account. If the source is unavailable, retain that limitation. The false-positive guide explains controls and ambiguous responses.

2. Use verified positive and negative controls

Compare a public account you control with a handle verified missing at the original service. A random-looking string can still belong to someone, so do not call it a guaranteed negative. Record the observation date and check found, not found and unknown separately. One false result is a detector problem to investigate; it does not establish that every historical result was false.

3. Read coverage as attempted scope

The standard release catalogue covers 500+ platforms. That does not guarantee every service responds reliably. Login walls, rate limits and platform changes can make checks unknown. Extended uses a separate 3,000+ catalogue. MyRecon has no direct Discord check, and no public username check unlocks private profiles.

4. Verify the current price and availability

Guests receive a limited username preview. Signed-in free accounts get five complete standard scans per UTC day. The optional one-time pack provides unlimited standard scans, paid Deep Search access and ten Extended scans, without automatic renewal. Indian checkout is ₹99; international checkout is US$3.99 where enabled. Read current plans and checkout details. The Android app is in closed testing for eligible testers; it is not generally available.

5. Review actual data handling

Browser storage, signed-in reports, hosting logs, analytics and third-party source requests are different data flows. Read the privacy policy before searching sensitive identifiers. Do not assume a tool is anonymous or retains nothing because a guest preview needs no account. Only use identifiers you own or are authorized to review.

6. Inspect published evidence and its limits

The homepage includes a recorded username comparison with captured evidence and uncertainty. Its small sample does not establish general accuracy, recall or superiority over every competitor. The source is available in the official repository; published source alone does not prove the deployed version or third-party behavior. You can report a minimal reproducible issue through Contact.

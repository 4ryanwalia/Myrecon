Have I Been Pwned describes this collection as scraped public profile data, not a breach of private LinkedIn data. That distinction matters when explaining how the records were obtained, even though their collection and circulation can still create privacy concerns.

The profile you made public was made public one reader at a time. That is a different thing from public data collected at scale and circulated. HIBP's record describes approximately 400 million records and 125 million unique email addresses; the two counts are not counts of individual people. Aggregated contact and employment details can help make phishing messages more targeted.

This is the same argument that runs through the 2021 Facebook scrape and the Clearview AI case file. Public and harmless are not the same word, and scale is what separates them.

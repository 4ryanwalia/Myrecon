LimeLeads sold B2B marketing leads, so, like many data brokers in this archive, most of the 17.8 million addresses in it belong to people who never dealt with the company. Their work email, direct phone number, employer, job title and location were compiled and sold for cold outreach, then left on an Elasticsearch server with no password.

An unsecured Elasticsearch database is one of the most familiar causes of data exposure and one of the least excusable. It is not a sophisticated attack; it is a database answering anyone who asks, and automated scanners that look for open servers find them quickly. The company is now defunct. The data is not: copies of leads databases are resold and merged long after the original seller has gone.

Breached in 2019 and added to Have I Been Pwned in 2026, this set is a reminder that "old" corporate contact data stays useful as long as people keep their jobs and phone numbers. If you are in it, the realistic consequence is more targeted cold calls and spear phishing at work, not account takeover. It is also a reason to opt out of the B2B data brokers that keep selling the same records.

The geographic fields are more precise than most corporate contact data, down to state, city and postcode. Combined with a job title and employer, that narrows a record to one office, which is useful to anyone planning an in-person pretext, such as a courier who needs a signature or a visitor claiming to be from IT, as well as to phone fraud. For most people that remains an unlikely use, but it is the reason security teams treat leaked lead lists as a reconnaissance resource rather than as spam.

The seven-year gap between the 2019 breach and the 2026 HIBP date is long even for this archive, and it shows how slowly leaked lead data surfaces publicly. Companies whose staff appear in it can use the HIBP domain search to see how many of their addresses are included, which is a quick way to judge how much of the organisation chart is already in circulation and which teams should expect targeted calls.

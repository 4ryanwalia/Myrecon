The name given to this entry is the take. It is filed as Not SOCRadar because the forum post claimed a cybersecurity firm had been breached, and the investigation that followed concluded something much duller: the data had been assembled using functionality the platform openly provides, gathering from publicly available sources. No intrusion, no failure, no stolen database.

Claims made while selling something are sales copy, and a hacking forum is a marketplace. Attaching a recognisable company's name to a pile of addresses raises both the price and the attention, and the cost of the claim lands on the company, which then spends weeks proving a negative. This is why an archive that records what was established separately from what was alleged is worth more than one that repeats the post.

If your address is in this set, the proportionate response is almost nothing. It says a mailbox of yours appears in a large scraped aggregate, most likely collected from sources that were already public. No provider you trusted failed, no password is implicated, and there is nothing to reset. Expect more spam, aimed slightly better. That is the entire consequence, and pretending otherwise would be the same inflation the original post was built on.

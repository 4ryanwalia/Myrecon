Credit scores, income levels, government-issued IDs and latitude and longitude pairs, in the same file. Each is serious on its own; together they describe a person's financial position and physical location precisely enough to select targets from the data itself.

That is the specific risk with lending platforms, and it is different from the risk at a retailer. A shop knows what you bought. A lender knows what you can afford, what you owe, how you were assessed and where you were when you applied. Fraud built on that is not scattergun, it is addressed to the people the file identifies as most likely to accept an offer of credit, using the detail that makes the offer look like it came from someone who already holds their business.

The government ID numbers are the part with no expiry date. A password is a weekend of work to replace; a national identity document number is not replaceable on request in most countries, and it will still be valid years from now. Where your jurisdiction offers a credit freeze or credit ban, this is the record that justifies using it, it blocks the specific fraud this combination of fields enables.

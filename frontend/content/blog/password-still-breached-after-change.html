If you changed your password and still see a breach warning, check where it appears. An email lookup can keep showing a past breach. A password manager may still have an old saved login or a second entry. An unfamiliar sign-in in the service's security history needs its own follow-up.

Why is my email still listed after a password reset?

A password reset updates the credential for that account. A breach catalogue keeps its historical record of which addresses appeared. Have I Been Pwned explicitly says its listing can remain after a password change. Have I Been Pwned FAQ

Look at the incident date, the date it became public and the data types the provider lists. A catalogue can add an old incident after your reset. For example, if a newsletter breach listed your email address and you changed the newsletter password last week, the address can still appear today. If you find that you reused the old password somewhere else, replace it there too. How to read breach dates

Why does Chrome still say a saved password is compromised?

A reset updates the account at the service. Checkup examines the logins stored in the password manager. Check whether you're looking at the same site, username and Chrome profile, especially if you use separate work and personal profiles. Chrome can save passwords to a Google Account or locally on a device. Google's guide to saved passwords

  1. Open the official service directly. Confirm that you can sign in to the correct account with the new password. Use the service's official recovery process if the sign-in fails.
  2. Open your password manager on the device that showed the alert. If multiple logins appear for the service, compare the site and usernames before updating the entry. Keep any entry that belongs to another account and review it separately.
  3. To update a saved entry in desktop Chrome, open Google Password Manager, select the matching entry, select Edit and save the verified new password. Then open Checkup again. Dismissing a warning only hides the alert. Chrome's saved-password steps · Google Password Checkup
  4. If the alert remains, check other entries and any separate profile or vault. Replace any exposed or reused password still in use with a unique one. Keep passwords and recovery codes out of your follow-up notes.

In 1Password, Compromised websites flags sites with a reported breach since the saved item's last password change. Vulnerable passwords flags saved passwords known to have been exposed. Check the category before deciding what still needs attention. 1Password Watchtower categories

What if the service shows a sign-in I don't recognize?

Open the service's security settings directly and review the event, devices and recovery details. Follow its steps for signing out unfamiliar sessions or restoring access. If you see unfamiliar Gmail activity, also review forwarding rules and connected apps. For a managed work or school account, contact the administrator through your usual support channel.

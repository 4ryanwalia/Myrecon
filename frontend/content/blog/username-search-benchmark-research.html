Methodology and evidence rules

  1. Use one explicitly supplied public handle. Run the standard MyRecon catalogue, Sherlock with its recorded upstream exclusions, Maigret's enabled unprotected username catalogue, and the separately observed OSINTsearch free preview. No customer history, private content, credentials or breach data enter this case study.
  2. Keep native outcomes. Preserve positive native verdicts, duplicates, normalized unknown outcomes and HTTP status codes. A tool's reported hit remains visible even when HTTP-error normalization quarantines it.
  3. Review sources separately. Use typed official API account objects, explicit missing responses, a different account handle, or separately recorded profile identity and missing-control observations. HTTP 200, agreement between tools, a challenge and a login wall do not establish existence. Parking destinations contradict a public-profile lead, without establishing whether an account ever existed.
  4. Score only supported evidence. Classify each unique reported address as supported, contradicted or unverified. Keep duplicates separate. Contradicted counts are observed lower bounds on false positives, and unresolved results remain unresolved. Do not infer common ownership.
  5. Compare both scopes. Show each complete recorded run and the exact-address intersection. Keep the hidden preview results outside address-level scoring.

Limitations and interpretation

  • Selection and publisher bias: one owner-requested handle and a small fixed control set do not represent all usernames, services or network conditions. MyRecon runs and publishes this comparison.
  • Missing truth and blocked checks: no contradicted reported leads is not a zero-error guarantee. Unknown engine outcomes can conceal missed profiles, and unverified leads may later be supported or contradicted.
  • Time and configuration: platform responses may change between scans and verification. Catalogue coverage, exclusions, endpoints, concurrency and overlapping local runs prevent a controlled speed ranking.
  • Corrections used this case: the final MyRecon run follows fixes informed by the observed handle mismatch and missing profiles. It is not an untouched, held-out evaluation. Earlier results are retained for review.
  • Hidden preview evidence: five OSINTsearch matches and all its profile URLs were hidden. No accuracy or identity inference is made for them.
  • Scope of a lead: a matching public handle does not confirm identity, common ownership, registration availability or access to private information.

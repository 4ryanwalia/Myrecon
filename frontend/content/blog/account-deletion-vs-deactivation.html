Is deactivating an account the same as deleting it?

Deactivation usually puts an account on hold and may hide the profile. Deletion starts permanent closure. On X and Snapchat, the deactivation period is already part of the deletion process.

Check public visibility separately from data retention. LinkedIn hides a hibernating profile but keeps its messages and comments under a generic label. Discord says it anonymizes the account across servers and messages after deletion. Copies that other people saved and records the provider retains follow their own rules.

What does deactivating your account do?

Deactivation hides or pauses the account without closing it. On Instagram, Discord and LinkedIn it is a separate option from deletion. On X and Snapchat it is the first stage of deletion, so the account is deleted if you do not return within 30 days.

The difference that matters is whether a clock is running:

  • A pause offered separately: Instagram's temporary deactivation, Discord's Disable Account and LinkedIn's hibernation are separate options from deletion, so choosing them is not the same as asking for the account to be closed. LinkedIn hides a hibernating profile, keeps its messages and comments under a generic label, and lets you reactivate after 24 hours.
  • A pause that becomes deletion: on X and Snapchat, deactivation is the first step of deleting. If you do not sign back in within the window, deletion goes ahead.
  • No deactivation at all: some services only offer deletion. GitHub's account documentation describes deleting, converting to an organization or merging into another account, but no temporary deactivation. See how to delete a GitHub account.

Deactivating does not remove what others already saved, and it does not cancel every subscription: X, for example, says subscriptions bought through an app store keep billing until you cancel them there.

What will my friends see if I deactivate my account?

On Facebook, no one else can see your profile, but friends may still see your name in their friends list and messages you sent. On Instagram, your photos, comments and likes are hidden. Both help pages were checked on 28 November 2026.

Facebook's deactivation page also says group admins may still see your posts and comments with your name, and Messenger keeps working if you stay logged in to it: "You can still chat with friends on Messenger." Deactivating Instagram hides content but is not a deletion request, and it can only be done once a week. How long can an Instagram account stay deactivated?

Instagram's help page on temporary deactivation, checked on 28 November 2026, gives no maximum period. It does say you can only deactivate your profile once a week, and that you need to be able to log in to do it.

Because the help page states no limit, MyRecon makes no claim about how many months or years a deactivated Instagram profile stays restorable. If you need the account kept, sign in before you rely on it. If you want it gone, use deletion rather than a long deactivation.

Do you have to deactivate an account before you can delete it?

X puts an account into deactivation when you start closing it. Snapchat starts deactivation when you choose Delete my account. Instagram and Discord offer separate choices, and LinkedIn calls its pause option hibernation.

So the statement "to delete an account permanently, you must deactivate it first" is false as a general rule. It describes how X and Snapchat work, where deactivation is built into deletion, but on Instagram, Discord and LinkedIn you choose deletion directly.

How to delete or recover an account

X

On the web, go to Settings and privacy, then Your account, then Deactivate your account. Download your archive before you start. X says subscriptions purchased through the app can continue billing and must be managed through the original billing platform. Subscriptions purchased on X.com cancel when you deactivate. X deactivation and billing instructions

Snapchat

Open the Snapchat accounts portal, choose Delete my account and confirm with your password. If you change your mind, sign in on the Snapchat app with your username. Snapchat says email and phone sign-in and password changes are unavailable while the account is deactivated. Export your data first if you want a copy. Snapchat deletion steps · Snapchat recovery instructions

Snapchat's dedicated articles describe 30 days to reactivate, followed by another 30 days before permanent deletion, or another 180 days in India. Its help overview describes the India total as 180 days after the request. Both agree on the first 30 days to recover. Use that earlier deadline if you want the account back. Snapchat help overview

Facebook

Facebook separates a temporary deactivation from permanent deletion. Its deletion page says you can cancel if it has been less than 30 days since you started, and "After 30 days, your account and all of your information will be permanently deleted." Removing everything you posted "may take up to 90 days", copies can stay in backup storage after that, and messages you sent stay in your friends' inboxes. You also lose Messenger. Facebook: permanently delete your profile

LinkedIn

Open Settings & Privacy, then Account preferences. Under Account management, select Close account and follow the confirmation prompts. Reopening within the allowed period can restore connections, while endorsements, recommendations, invitations and group memberships can stay lost. To keep your profile and hide it temporarily, choose Hibernate account instead. LinkedIn closure instructions · LinkedIn reopening rules · LinkedIn hibernation rules

Discord

On the web or desktop, open User Settings, then My Account, then Delete Account. Transfer or delete any servers you own first. If you change your mind, sign in and choose Restore Account. Discord's account instructions give 15 days, while its broader retention article describes a typical 15–30 day hold. Use the 15-day deadline from the instructions for restoring your account. Discord deletion steps · Discord recovery instructions · Discord retention policy

GitHub

Open Settings, then Account, and under Delete account choose Delete your account, then type your username or email and the confirmation phrase. There is no deactivation step and no grace period: GitHub says it cannot restore your content once the account is deleted, and your username becomes available to anyone after 90 days. How long is data kept after you delete an account?

It depends on the platform, and the time you can undo a deletion is shorter than the time some data can be kept. Facebook, for example, gives 30 days to cancel but up to 90 days to remove everything you posted.

In the provider instructions above, you can restore a Discord account within 15 days, an X or Snapchat account within 30 days, and usually reopen a LinkedIn account within 14 days. Discord's retention article describes a typical hold of 15 to 30 days, and Snapchat's own articles add another 30 days after its recovery window before permanent deletion, or longer in India.

A deletion request applies to the account and data covered by the provider's policy. Some records may remain in backups or for legal, security or billing purposes. Screenshots, reposts, archives and search results can remain visible through other sites.

Check these things before either choice

  • Download photos, messages or files you want to keep. Instagram, for example, says its export tool is unavailable once the profile is deleted.
  • Check active subscriptions with the provider that bills you and cancel any that would continue.
  • Move any other account that uses this profile to sign in or recover access.
  • Transfer ownership of groups, pages, servers, projects or shared files you want to keep available.

Should you deactivate or delete?

If you only want a break, choose the service's pause option after checking whether it starts a deletion countdown. If you want to leave, save the deletion confirmation and the last day you can cancel. To reduce what strangers see while keeping the account, start by removing details from the public profile and checking your privacy settings.

Can a deleted account be recovered?

You may be able to restore an account while deletion is pending. Follow the platform's recovery steps before the stated deadline. Some services require a separate confirmation after you sign in, and reopening can leave some data unrestored.

Is deactivating an account the same as deleting it?

Deactivation usually puts an account on hold and may hide the profile. Deletion starts permanent closure. On X and Snapchat, the deactivation period is already part of the deletion process.

Check public visibility separately from data retention. LinkedIn hides a hibernating profile but keeps its messages and comments under a generic label. Discord says it anonymizes the account across servers and messages after deletion. Copies that other people saved and records the provider retains follow their own rules.

Do you have to deactivate an account before you can delete it?

X puts an account into deactivation when you start closing it. Snapchat starts deactivation when you choose Delete my account. Instagram and Discord offer separate choices, and LinkedIn calls its pause option hibernation.

How to delete or recover an account

X

On the web, go to Settings and privacy, then Your account, then Deactivate your account. Download your archive before you start. X says subscriptions purchased through the app can continue billing and must be managed through the original billing platform. Subscriptions purchased on X.com cancel when you deactivate. X deactivation and billing instructions

Snapchat

Open the Snapchat accounts portal, choose Delete my account and confirm with your password. If you change your mind, sign in on the Snapchat app with your username. Snapchat says email and phone sign-in and password changes are unavailable while the account is deactivated. Export your data first if you want a copy. Snapchat deletion steps · Snapchat recovery instructions

Snapchat's dedicated articles describe 30 days to reactivate, followed by another 30 days before permanent deletion, or another 180 days in India. Its help overview describes the India total as 180 days after the request. Both agree on the first 30 days to recover. Use that earlier deadline if you want the account back. Snapchat help overview

LinkedIn

Open Settings & Privacy, then Account preferences. Under Account management, select Close account and follow the confirmation prompts. Reopening within the allowed period can restore connections, while endorsements, recommendations, invitations and group memberships can stay lost. To keep your profile and hide it temporarily, choose Hibernate account instead. LinkedIn closure instructions · LinkedIn reopening rules · LinkedIn hibernation rules

Discord

On the web or desktop, open User Settings, then My Account, then Delete Account. Transfer or delete any servers you own first. If you change your mind, sign in and choose Restore Account. Discord's account instructions give 15 days, while its broader retention article describes a typical 15–30 day hold. Use the 15-day deadline from the instructions for restoring your account. How long is data kept after you delete an account?

It depends on the platform, and the time you can undo a deletion is shorter than the time some data can be kept. In the provider instructions above, you can restore a Discord account within 15 days, an X or Snapchat account within 30 days, and usually reopen a LinkedIn account within 14 days. Discord's retention article describes a typical hold of 15 to 30 days, and Snapchat's own articles add another 30 days after its recovery window before permanent deletion, or longer in India.

A deletion request applies to the account and data covered by the provider's policy. Some records may remain in backups or for legal, security or billing purposes. Screenshots, reposts, archives and search results can remain visible through other sites.

To check a lingering Google result, open the exact profile link while signed out. If the profile still shows the information, start with the platform that hosts it. Check these things before either choice

  • Download photos, messages or files you want to keep. Instagram, for example, says its export tool is unavailable once the profile is deleted.
  • Check active subscriptions with the provider that bills you and cancel any that would continue.
  • Move any other account that uses this profile to sign in or recover access.
  • Transfer ownership of groups, pages, servers, projects or shared files you want to keep available.

Should you deactivate or delete?

If you only want a break, choose the service's pause option after checking whether it starts a deletion countdown. If you want to leave, save the deletion confirmation and the last day you can cancel. To reduce what strangers see while keeping the account, start by removing details from the public profile and checking your privacy settings.

Can a deleted account be recovered?

You may be able to restore an account while deletion is pending. Follow the platform's recovery steps before the stated deadline. Some services require a separate confirmation after you sign in, and reopening can leave some data unrestored.

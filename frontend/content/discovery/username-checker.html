Try a browser username search step by step

  1. Choose one known handle. Enter a username you own or are authorized to research. Use an old spelling separately if you remember changing it.
  2. Choose an available scope. As a guest, select Quick and press Investigate. Sign in before choosing Standard for the larger catalogue and complete result cards.
  3. Inspect the labels. Open visible profile links and compare the exact handle with the source page. Keep a found profile separate from confirmed ownership.
  4. Resolve your own accounts. Check saved logins or signup records and sign in through the provider. Record an account action only after confirming control.

MyRecon is useful when you have a handle and want several public-source leads in one browser session. For a single known service, opening its official profile directly may be quicker. A local Sherlock workflow may fit better when you need repeatable terminal commands.

What a username search can leave unresolved

Private pages, login requirements, challenges, rate limits, renamed handles and unsupported services can leave gaps. An unknown check is not a negative result. A missing public profile does not guarantee that a name is available to register; confirm availability in the platform's own signup or settings flow.

Matching handles, photos and display names can belong to unrelated people or be copied. Use the account-match verification guide. For your own cleanup, continue with forgotten-account discovery, a digital-footprint review or a separate email-breach check.

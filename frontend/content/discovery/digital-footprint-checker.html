Choose a question you can act on

A useful first goal is “find old profiles where I still publish contact details” or “review services named in my email's breach results.” A scan cannot inventory every private account, tracking record, data-broker entry, server log or copy of your information.

MyRecon is useful for collecting public leads across supported sources in a browser. Use your password manager, inbox and provider settings for records that a public checker cannot see. A footprint review needs both, especially when a handle has changed or a service has no public profile.

Run a footprint check in four passes

  1. Build a private starting list. Record current and remembered handles, email addresses and recognized services. Keep passwords, recovery codes and identity documents out of your audit notes.
  2. Search public handles. Use the browser username workflow, open candidate URLs and note what a signed-out visitor can see. Confirm your own account through saved records or a recognized provider session.
  3. Review email exposure separately. Follow the email-breach guide. A named dataset can prompt a security review; it does not establish an active account or a leaked current password.
  4. Make and verify one change. Edit a public bio, update account security or follow the provider's deletion process. Reopen the source in a signed-out window and record whether the exposed detail changed.

Current access limits for a footprint review

Username checks: guests get 5 Quick scans per day across 100 platforms with only the first 2 result cards visible. Signed-in free accounts get 5 Standard scans daily across 500+ platforms; Quick and Standard share this allowance. Daily resets are midnight UTC (05:30 India).

Email checks: returned findings are visible without sign-in or payment and do not spend the username allowance. Optional sources may be unconfigured, blocked, rate-limited or unavailable. Check the source status before interpreting an empty result.

Paid username scope: the optional pack adds unlimited Standard, Deep Search and 10 Extended scans across 3,000+ platforms. A larger catalogue does not authenticate account ownership or grant private access. See current pricing and credit rules; Android access is described in the app.

Prioritize changes you can verify

  • Account security: replace a reused password, review sessions and recovery settings, and enable available stronger sign-in protection.
  • Public details: remove an unnecessary phone number, old employer or location from a profile you control, then inspect the signed-out page.
  • Forgotten accounts: confirm control, export needed data, settle billing and linked logins, then use the deletion checklist.
  • Other sites and search results: record the exact URL and request correction or removal through the relevant process. Track submitted and completed separately.

Google's personal-information removal guidance distinguishes removing a result from Search from removing the source page. Search removal can leave the original online. MyRecon does not automatically delete accounts or guarantee complete erasure.

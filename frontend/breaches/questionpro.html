MyRecon's take

"Survey results" is the quietest data class in this archive and one of the most revealing. People answer surveys candidly precisely because they believe the answers are aggregated and anonymous. Tie those answers back to an email address and an IP address, as this data does, and the assumption that made them candid is the thing that makes the file valuable.

The employee engagement survey, the health questionnaire, the political polling, the customer satisfaction form that asked why you left, all of it is opinion given under an expectation of distance from the person holding it.

Worth noting too how this record's status changed. It sat flagged as unverified because the company would not confirm a breach, and the flag was removed only after people found their own accurate records in it. A company declining to confirm does not make a dataset false, and the verification eventually came from the people in it rather than from the organisation holding it.

What happened

In May 2022, the survey website QuestionPro was the target of an extortion attempt relating to an alleged data breach. Over 100GB of data containing 22M unique email addresses (some of which appear to be generated by the platform), are alleged to have been extracted from the service along with IP addresses, browser user agents and results relating to surveys. QuestionPro would not confirm whether a breach had occurred (although they did confirm they were the target of an extortion attempt), so the data was initially flagged as "unverified". Subsequent verification by impacted HIBP subscribers later led to the removal of the unverified flag.

Who was behind it

No party has been publicly confirmed as responsible, and this page will not name one. Most breaches are never formally attributed: data surfaces on a forum or inside a combined dump long after the intrusion, and the trail back to a specific actor is rarely made public. Where a group has claimed responsibility it is usually named in the account above, that claim is theirs, not a finding of ours.

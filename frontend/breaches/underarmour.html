MyRecon's take

The size is not the interesting part. What matters here is the two-month gap: the ransomware group claimed the intrusion in November, and the data only reached a public forum in January. For anyone in that file, the exposure began long before there was anything to read about it.

That gap is the norm rather than the exception, which is why "we have seen no evidence of misuse" in a disclosure statement means considerably less than it sounds like.

The story behind the breach

In November 2025, the Everest ransomware group claimed Under Armour as a victim and attempted to extort a ransom , alleging they had obtained access to 343GB of data. In January 2026, customer data from the incident was published publicly on a popular hacking forum , including 72M email addresses. Many records also contained additional personal information such as names, dates of birth, genders, geographic locations and purchase information.

Breach description from Have I Been Pwned, used under a CC BY 4.0 licence. Data provided to HIBP by DeHashed.

What was exposed, and why it matters

Dates of birth+

One of the three things call centres ask to verify identity, and it never changes.

Email addresses+

The address becomes a confirmed, active target, expect more phishing, better aimed.

Genders+

Small on its own; useful to anyone assembling a fuller profile.

Geographic locations+

Where the account was used, which narrows a person down further than most people expect.

Names+

Turns an anonymous address into an identified person, which is what makes targeted phishing possible.

Purchases+

What was bought and when, detail that makes an impersonation convincing.

When did it happen, and when did it become public?

The breach is dated 17 November 2025. It was published to Have I Been Pwned on 21 January 2026, a gap of 65 days during which the data was already out.

Is the Under Armour breach real?

Yes. Have I Been Pwned lists it as verified, meaning the data was checked against the source rather than taken on trust.

How many people were affected?

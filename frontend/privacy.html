Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 July 2026

No accounts

MyRecon does not require registration and does not collect names, passwords, or payment details.

Your searches

Search targets are sent to the MyRecon API only to perform the lookup you request. We do not persist your search queries or results to a database. Results may be held in a short-lived in-memory cache (typically a few minutes) purely to accelerate repeated lookups, after which they expire automatically.

Search history

Your recent-lookup history is stored exclusively in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time from the tool.

Third-party sources

To answer a lookup, the API queries public data providers (for example RDAP registries, public DNS resolvers, breach databases, and platform pages). Those providers receive the specific target you queried as part of the request.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the address published at myrecon.xyz.

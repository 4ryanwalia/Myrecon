MyRecon App for Android

MyRecon is an Android app for public username search, digital footprint research and breach checks. Run username checks from your phone and review the source links.

Download free on Google Play Try the browser preview

Android 11 or newer · Contains ads · Core tools need no MyRecon account.

Why on-device matters

The standard username sweep makes requests to public sources from your phone. Sites may still require a login, rate-limit requests or return an ambiguous response. Review the source link before relying on a match.

What it does

Username sweep

Social, developer, gaming, marketplace, forum and portfolio sites, checked concurrently. The app distinguishes evidence-backed profile leads from unverified and rate-limited checks. A profile found under the same username needs separate evidence of ownership.

Breach alerts, without handing over your address

Breach Watch can check the public breach catalogue on your phone without submitting your email address. An optional address-specific lookup uses a separate switch with its own disclosure.

QR link safety

Scan a QR code and see where it actually goes before you open it, the full redirect chain, the final destination, and whether anything about it looks like a lookalike domain.

Password leak check

The app hashes your password on the phone and sends the first five characters of that hash to Have I Been Pwned. It checks the returned candidates on your device. How Pwned Passwords handles hash ranges.

Domain, DNS and IP lookups

Registration details over RDAP, mail authentication read from the domain's own published SPF, DKIM and DMARC records, subdomains recovered from Certificate Transparency logs, and geolocation for an address. Every source is public and keyless.

Identity correlation

Accounts that appear to belong to the same person are grouped, with a confidence score and the signals behind it. A matching handle alone is never treated as proof of identity.

Availability

MyRecon is free to download on Google Play and requires Android 11 or newer. Deep Search shows its own unlock requirements in the app. You can also use the browser username preview without installing anything.

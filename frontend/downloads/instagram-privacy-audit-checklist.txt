[ ] Check who can see your posts and stories. Review your follower list if access is restricted.
[ ] Review who can tag or mention you, and whether tagged posts need your approval.
[ ] Review who can send message requests or add you to group chats.
[ ] Check contact syncing and remove personal contact details from your public profile if needed.
[ ] Review login activity and sign out of devices you no longer recognise.
[ ] Confirm you can reach your recovery email or phone and verify that two-factor authentication is enabled.
[ ] Recheck your profile after saving changes. Mark the public view as unavailable if Instagram asks you to sign in.

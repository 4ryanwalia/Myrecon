## Appendix B: Confidence scale

- Confirmed: verified at the original source and supported by at least one independent source.
- Probable: verified at the original source, with consistent but not independent support.
- Possible: a single unverified signal, such as a matching username.
- Unresolved: conflicting, ambiguous or unavailable evidence.

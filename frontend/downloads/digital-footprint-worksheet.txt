Save your completed copy privately. Do not record passwords, recovery codes,
government IDs or private messages. Use this for accounts you own or are
authorised to manage. A matching handle alone does not establish ownership.

Copy the following entry for each account or source page:

Service or source:
Exact public URL (leave out private tokens):
Reviewed on:
Ownership: unconfirmed / confirmed mine / authorised to manage / not mine
Basis for confirmation (short note, no secrets):
Public detail to change:
Desired action: review / edit visibility / export / close / request removal
Before closing: check billing, exports and dependent sign-ins
Action taken on:
Provider status: not started / submitted / confirmed / declined / unresolved
Source page checked on:
Source outcome:
Search result status: not checked / still present / updated / removed
Follow-up date:
Next action:

FINISHING A REVIEW
[ ] Keep unconfirmed profiles separate from your confirmed account inventory.
[ ] Verify changes at the original source.
[ ] Treat search-result removal and source deletion as separate tasks.
[ ] Schedule a follow-up where a provider is still processing a request.
[ ] Delete notes you no longer need.

No checker can guarantee a complete account inventory or deletion of every copy.

Frequently asked questions about the video walkthrough

What is shown in the MyRecon product walkthrough video?

The walkthrough demonstrates running multi-platform username sweeps across 500+ sources, checking email breach exposures with k-anonymity, auditing digital footprints, and performing privacy cleanup without command-line dependencies.

How does MyRecon prevent false positives during username searches?

As shown in the video, MyRecon tests synthetic negative controls to detect platform false positives, wildcard matching, and HTTP 200 soft-404 redirects before returning verified public profile leads.

Can I try the search features shown in the video for free?

Yes. Guests can run free Quick sweeps without creating an account or providing payment information. Standard scans across 500+ platforms are available with a free account.

Where is the video hosted?

The product walkthrough video is embedded using YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com), which does not set tracking cookies until the user initiates playback.

How can I audit or remove accounts found during a search?

MyRecon provides direct links to official platform deletion pages, opt-out request forms for data brokers, and step-by-step privacy audit guides in our resource library.

Consent-based self-audits and privacy ethics

MyRecon is designed to help you understand and reduce your own public exposure or conduct authorized security reviews. A matching handle is a lead to verify, not proof of identity. Review our terms of use and privacy policy.

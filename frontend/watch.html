Frequently asked questions about the video walkthrough

How long is the MyRecon product video?

The MyRecon product video runs for 60 seconds. Use the current product pages and guides for detailed workflows, supported checks and plan limits.

Does a username match prove who owns an account?

No. A matching handle is a public-profile lead. Open the original source and compare independent evidence before attributing the profile to someone. Blocks, timeouts and ambiguous responses can leave a check unknown.

Can I try the search features shown in the video for free?

Yes. Guests can run free Quick sweeps without creating an account or providing payment information. Standard scans across 500+ platforms are available with a free account.

Where is the video hosted?

This page embeds YouTube's privacy-enhanced player at youtube-nocookie.com. YouTube provides playback; review the relevant privacy policies before watching.

How can I audit or remove accounts found during a search?

First confirm which accounts are yours. Review their public details and use the official service settings to secure, change or delete them. MyRecon's privacy guides explain the process and its limits; a search result does not automatically remove an account.

Consent-based self-audits and privacy ethics

MyRecon is designed to help you understand and reduce your own public exposure or conduct authorized security reviews. A matching handle is a lead to verify, not proof of identity. Review our terms of use and privacy policy.

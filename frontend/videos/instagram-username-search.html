Check an Instagram username

Use an exact handle and the official Instagram destination while keeping unavailable profiles and account ownership unresolved.

32 seconds. Silent explainer with on-screen text and English captions.

Video transcript

  1. Copy the exact handle. Keep dots, underscores and digits. A display name is different from a username.
  2. Use the official profile. Open Instagram search or the profile destination and confirm the domain.
  3. Describe your access. Record a visible page, a login wall or an unavailable destination without guessing the cause.
  4. Confirm the association. Use an owner-published link or your own account records. A handle alone does not prove identity.

Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026 · Applies to Pro passes bought on myrecon.xyz

The short version

  • Nothing renews. Pro passes are one-time payments, so there is no subscription to cancel.
  • Changed your mind? Full refund within 7 days of purchase if you have not used any of that pass’s scans.
  • Charged twice, or paid and the pass never appeared? Full refund or the pass applied, whichever you prefer.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay, usually within 5 to 7 working days.

1. What you are buying

A Pro pass is a digital service: a number of full (560-platform) username scans usable for a fixed period, Pro Weekly (₹99, 50 scans, 7 days) or Pro Monthly (₹299, 200 scans, 30 days). The full terms are in section 12 of our terms of use. Everything else on MyRecon is free and needs no payment.

2. Delivery

Nothing is shipped. A pass is applied to your signed-in account as soon as Razorpay confirms the payment, normally within seconds and at most within an hour. If it has not appeared on the pricing page after an hour, write to us with your Razorpay payment ID and we will apply it or refund you.

3. Cancellation

Passes do not auto-renew and nothing is charged again unless you choose to buy again, so there is nothing to cancel. You can stop using a pass at any time; unused scans simply expire at the end of its period.

4. When you get a refund

Refund outcomes.

A scan that fails because of a fault on our side is returned to your allowance automatically and never counts as used.

5. How to ask

Email aryan@bugsnaps.in from the address you signed in with, and include the Razorpay payment ID from your receipt. We reply within 2 working days. Approved refunds are issued through Razorpay to the original payment method; your bank or card issuer usually shows them within 5 to 7 working days.

This policy does not affect any right you have under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 or other law that cannot be waived.

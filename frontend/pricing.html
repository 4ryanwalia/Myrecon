How it works

Passes are one-time payments through Razorpay (UPI, cards, net banking and wallets). Nothing renews, so there is nothing to cancel. Buying another pass while one is active adds its scans and extends the end date. A full scan is counted when it starts; if it fails on our side, it is given back. Refunds are covered in the refund and cancellation policy.

Why the full scan is limited

A full scan makes over a thousand requests: each of 560 platforms, plus a comparison request against an impossible handle wherever a platform's answer is ambiguous. That is what lets it say "no account" only with evidence. It runs on our server, so the number we can run at once is finite. The Android app runs the same sweep from your phone instead.

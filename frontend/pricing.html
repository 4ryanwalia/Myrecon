Pricing

Every tool on MyRecon is free to start. Guest login needs no sign-up. Quick scans check 100 platforms and show the first 2 result cards; the rest are blurred. Standard requires sign-in. Signed-in free accounts get 5 standard 500+ platform scans a day with complete results. The paid pack is ₹99 through Indian checkout, or US$3.99 through PayPal or Buy Me a Coffee when the chosen checkout is enabled, for unlimited standard scans, Deep Search and 10 Extended scans across 3,000+ platforms. Email lookup is free for everyone. All returned breach, profile and optional linked-account findings are visible without sign-in or a paid plan. Email lookups do not use your username scan allowance. Source outages can still leave a lookup incomplete.

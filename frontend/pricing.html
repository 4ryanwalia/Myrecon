How scan allowances work

Signed-in free accounts get 5 standard scans daily, resetting at midnight UTC. Pro includes unlimited standard scans and Deep Search during the trial or paid period. Subscription credits reset each billing period and do not roll over. Buy Me a Coffee packs keep their existing non-expiring credits and unlimited standard access. An Extended scan uses one credit when it starts; if it fails on our side, the credit is returned. Reopening a saved report does not count toward the daily allowance. Cached standard requests count toward the 5 daily scans; cached Extended results do not spend a credit. Cached Extended results still require an available credit. Previously purchased passes retain their remaining Extended scans and original expiry. See our terms and refund policy.

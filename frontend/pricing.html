How it works

Passes are one-time payments through Razorpay (UPI, cards, net banking and wallets). Nothing renews, so there is nothing to cancel. Buying another pass while one is active adds its scans and extends the end date. A full or Extended scan is counted when it starts; if it fails on our side, it is given back. Extended scans come only with a pass and have their own allowance. All sales are final; see the refund and cancellation policy.

Why the full scan is limited

A full scan makes over a thousand requests: each of the 500+ platforms, plus a comparison request against an impossible handle wherever a platform's answer is ambiguous. That is what lets it say "no account" only with evidence. It runs on our server, so the number we can run at once is finite. The Android app runs the same sweep from your phone instead.

Frequently asked questions

What does MyRecon provide for OSINT lookup pricing?

MyRecon provides product functions described on its current website, including a public username lookup across supported checks. Exact scope, access, and plan details can change; review the live interface and verify each candidate at its original source.

Does a missing username result prove no account exists?

No. A site may block, rate-limit, rename, or restrict a profile, and some services may not be supported. Treat an unavailable or missing result as inconclusive unless the source provides a verifiable reason.

Can MyRecon see private accounts?

No. Public username checks do not grant access to private accounts or restricted content. Use service-approved access and consent, and do not bypass login walls or platform controls to complete a search.

Know what happens next

What happens after the 3-day trial?

Your selected subscription starts automatically unless you cancel before the trial ends: US$3.99/month or US$19.99/year with PayPal; ₹299/month or ₹1,999/year with Razorpay. The annual price is charged in full each year. Check the exact first charge date at checkout. One trial per account.

How do I cancel?

Sign in to this page, open Manage Pro, and select Cancel subscription. Confirmed access continues until the current trial or paid period ends. Cancel before the displayed next charge to stop renewal. Read the cancellation policy.

Should I choose monthly or annual?

Choose monthly for a smaller recurring commitment. Choose annual for regular research: US$19.99 instead of US$47.88 for 12 monthly payments (58% less), or ₹1,999 instead of ₹3,588 (44% less). Annual billing includes 120 Extended scans per paid year; monthly includes 10 per paid month. Both trials include 3.

Can I try MyRecon without payment details?

Yes. Run a free Quick scan, or sign in for 5 Standard scans a day. The Pro trial requires payment authorization. For occasional Extended scans, the one-time pack above is another option.

How scan allowances work

Signed-in free accounts get 5 standard scans daily, resetting at midnight UTC. Pro includes unlimited standard scans and Deep Search during the trial or paid period. Subscription credits reset each billing period and do not roll over. Buy Me a Coffee packs keep their existing non-expiring credits and unlimited standard access. An Extended scan uses one credit when it starts; if it fails on our side, the credit is returned. Scan inputs, results and history are not saved to Firebase or server storage. Each new Extended scan spends a credit; results stay on the current page. Previously purchased passes retain their remaining Extended scans and original expiry. See our terms and refund policy.
